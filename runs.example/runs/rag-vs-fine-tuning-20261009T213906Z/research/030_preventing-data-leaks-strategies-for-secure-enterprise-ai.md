# Preventing Data Leaks: Strategies for Secure Enterprise AI

Generative AI has become the centerpiece of digital innovation, promising transformative outcomes for enterprises. Yet, high-profile incidents remind us that achieving this potential hinges on securely retrieving and managing data.

Consider the [Microsoft Copilot incident](https://www.businessinsider.com/microsoft-copilot-oversharing-problem-fix-customers-2024-11), where improperly managed access controls exposed sensitive enterprise data. Similarly, Google Bard [faltered in a promotional campaign](https://www.reuters.com/technology/google-ai-chatbot-bard-offers-inaccurate-information-company-ad-2023-02-08/), producing inaccurate responses that damaged its credibility. ChatGPT, too, has faced scrutiny for fabricating plausible-sounding but false information, even leading to [real-world legal ramifications](https://apnews.com/article/artificial-intelligence-chatgpt-fake-case-lawyers-d6ae9fa79d0542db9e1455397aef381c).

These missteps highlight a common thread: even the most advanced [enterprise AI](https://www.coveo.com/blog/enterprise-ai/) systems struggle when their foundations — data governance and secure retrieval — are weak. But they also reveal an opportunity: with the right infrastructure and approach, enterprises can avoid these pitfalls and unlock generative AI’s transformative potential.

## Solving the Secure Retrieval Problem: Lessons from the Field

Coveo’s platform is specifically designed to tackle the challenges of secure, relevance-driven data retrieval in [generative AI](https://www.coveo.com/blog/what-is-generative-ai/). Here’s how we address the risks illustrated by these high-profile cases:

### 1. Relevance-Augmented Retrieval: The Antidote to Hallucinations

AI tools like ChatGPT and Bard often hallucinate — producing incorrect or irrelevant outputs — due to poor data retrieval. [Coveo’s relevance-augmented retrieval](https://www.wired.com/sponsored/story/coveo/), combined with strict prompt engineering, ensures generative responses are grounded in secure, up-to-date, and contextually relevant content. This minimizes [hallucinations](https://www.coveo.com/blog/what-are-ai-hallucinations/) and enhances trust in AI outputs.

### 2. No Answer is Often the Best Answer: Ensuring Accuracy Over Guesswork

In the realm of AI, providing an answer when grounded information is unavailable can lead to false answers or hallucinations. Coveo’s approach prioritizes accuracy by ensuring that if the relevant data is not accessible, the system refrains from generating a response. This practice not only prevents the dissemination of incorrect information but also builds trust by demonstrating a commitment to reliability and truthfulness in AI outputs.

### 3. Document-Level Security to Prevent Data Exposure

The Microsoft Copilot incident revealed how weak access controls can lead to data leaks. Good data governance is essential to maintain the integrity and security of data. Additionally, a retrieval system like Coveo, which enforces document-level permissions at the core of its platform, can ensure that sensitive data remains secure, even in highly integrated environments.

### 4. Unified Data Layers for Seamless Integration

Enterprises often struggle with fragmented data across CRM, CMS, and other systems. This fragmentation makes secure retrieval difficult. Coveo consolidates these data sources into a single secure layer, simplifying generative AI integration while maintaining strict compliance.
